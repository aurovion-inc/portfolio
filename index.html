What works, and why.

The same element, built two ways. The difference is rarely taste; usually it's something you can measure.

Contrast

4.5:1 for body text

Muted text is a hierarchy tool. Below 4.5:1 against its background it stops being quiet and starts being unreadable.

Hierarchy

1 primary action

When three buttons shout, people stop and compare. Give one action the weight and step the others down.

Proximity

closer means related

Spacing groups things before borders do. A label has to sit closer to its own field than to the one above it.

Type scale

one ratio, 1.333

Sizes one pixel apart read as mistakes, not hierarchy. Take every size from one scale and let weight and color do the rest.

Not color alone

color plus words

About 1 in 12 men has some form of color blindness. Pair color with words or a shape, and say how to fix the problem.

Touch targets

44 px on touch

Apple recommends 44 by 44 points for anything tappable, and WCAG 2.2 sets 24 px as the floor. Small, tight targets cause mis-taps.

Color, on purpose.

This page runs on neutrals and a single accent, split roughly 60, 30 and 10. The accent means something because it's rare.

  • 60% background and surfaces
  • 30% text and lines
  • 10% accent, only where you should act

      Finished, not just done.

      A screen is done when the happy path works. It's finished when every state is designed: empty, loading, error, and the name that's three times longer than in the mockup.
